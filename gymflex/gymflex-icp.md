# GymFlex — Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

## Primary Customer

**Who:** Active adults aged 22–40 who train 3–5 times per week and care about how they look and perform in and out of the gym.

**Mindset:** They don't just buy gym clothes — they buy into a routine. Training, recovery, and consistency matter. They follow fitness creators on Instagram and TikTok and discover brands through ads and influencer content.

---

## Demographics

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Age | 22–40 |
| Gender | Men and women (slight skew toward women in lifestyle/athleisure discovery) |
| Income | Middle to upper-middle |
| Location | Urban and suburban, US-first |
| Platforms | Instagram, TikTok, Facebook |

---

## Psychographics

- Values **performance + aesthetics** — wants gear that works and looks good
- Motivated by progress, discipline, and community
- Buys based on social proof, creator endorsements, and clean brand visuals
- Responds to short, punchy messaging (Recover Reset Repeat)

---

## Pain Points

- Cheap gym wear that loses shape or looks dated quickly
- Brands that feel too masculine-only or too generic
- Logos and designs that don't look premium on camera
- Apparel that doesn't transition from workout to daily wear

---

## What They Want From GymFlex

- Premium feel at a reachable price
- Clean, modern branding they recognize instantly
- Apparel that looks good in mirror selfies and gym content
- A brand that speaks to **consistency** — not just one intense workout

---

## Logo & Brand Implications

The logo should appeal to this ICP by feeling:

- **Modern** — not retro gym bro, not corporate wellness
- **Bold but simple** — readable in a TikTok thumbnail or on a hoodie chest print
- **Athletic** — suggests movement, strength, or flexibility without cliché dumbbells
- **Premium** — could sit next to Gymshark/Alo/Lululemon without looking out of place
