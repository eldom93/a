# GymFlex — Business Deep Dive

## Company Overview

**GymFlex** is a premium fitness apparel brand built for people who train hard and recover smarter. The brand sits at the intersection of performance, comfort, and modern lifestyle — not just gym clothes, but everyday athletic wear that moves with you.

**Tagline:** Recover. Reset. Repeat.

**Supporting message:** Performance wear that moves with you.

---

## Brand Positioning

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| Category | Premium fitness / athleisure apparel |
| Price tier | Mid-to-premium (accessible luxury) |
| Aesthetic | Clean, modern, athletic, confident |
| Comparable brands | Gymshark, Alo Yoga, Lululemon |
| Tone | Motivating, disciplined, aspirational but approachable |

---

## Product Focus

- Performance hoodies, leggings, shorts, sports bras, and training tops
- Recovery-focused messaging (rest, reset, repeat the work)
- Fabrics that emphasize stretch, breathability, and durability
- Designed for training, lifestyle, and social content

---

## Visual Identity Direction

- **Style:** Minimal, bold, modern — works on apparel, ads, and digital
- **Feel:** Athletic energy without clutter; premium without being corporate
- **Use cases:** Instagram/TikTok ads, website, packaging, business cards, app icon
- **Logo requirements:** Readable at small sizes; works on light and dark backgrounds; ideally delivered as transparent PNG

---

## Brand Personality

- Confident, not arrogant
- Performance-driven
- Inclusive athletic energy (men and women)
- Disciplined and consistent — the "repeat" in Recover Reset Repeat

---

## Key Messages for Creative

1. Train with intention
2. Recovery is part of performance
3. Gear that keeps up with real movement
4. Built for the feed and the floor — social-native, gym-ready
