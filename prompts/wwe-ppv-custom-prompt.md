## The 8 things every good image prompt needs

You only need to define these:

1. **What the model is doing** — pose and action
2. **Wardrobe** — clothing and accessories
3. **Expression** — emotion
4. **Setting** — location and event
5. **Camera angle & composition** — height, tilt, distance, framing
6. **Lens & camera settings** — focal length, aperture, shutter, ISO
7. **Lighting & atmosphere** — spotlights, haze, rim light, color
8. **Environment details** — arena, barricade, crowd, ring elements

Optional: depth/focus and visual style.

- Spotlights converging behind the subject create a natural halo effect

- **ChatGPT** — generally higher quality faces and overall coherence

- Don't pay for "150 best prompts" — ask AI to generate prompt lists, then customize with reference analysis

## The workflow (what you're actually learning)

> *"Good artists copy; great artists steal."* — Picasso

You're not learning to paste blindly. You're learning to:

1. Use a **proven template** as scaffolding
2. Attach a **reference image** for identity
3. **Research a shot** you want to replicate
4. Let **AI decode the camera language** for you
5. **Adapt the scene** while keeping what works (lighting, angle, composition)
6. **Iterate across tools** until you get the result

You don't need to be a designer, photographer, or director. You need references, AI analysis, and a structured prompt.
